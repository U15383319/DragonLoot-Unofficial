# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Extend a successful dragon axe shield disable by 20 ticks by default. The dragon shield applies its cooldown multiplier to the combined duration.
- Use Riptide with the dragon trident in modded liquids.
- Update the dragon shield and Dragon Upgrade Smithing Template textures.
- Duplicate the upgrade template with seven popped chorus fruit and an obsidian block.

## 1.0.0

First release of DragonLoot Onyx for Minecraft 1.19.2 and Forge 43.x.

- Collect dragon scales from the Ender Dragon to upgrade armor, tools, weapons, horse armor, and anvils.
- Find the Dragon Upgrade Smithing Template in End City chests and duplicate it with amethyst shards and a purpur block.
- Use a dragon shield with a shorter disable cooldown, a dragon sword with extra melee reach, and a dragon chestplate that can be combined with an elytra.
- Adjust drops, gear stats, and combat bonuses with the optional Cloth Config screen.
