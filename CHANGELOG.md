# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release of DragonLoot Onyx for Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge 21.1.

- Collect dragon scales from the Ender Dragon to upgrade armor, tools, weapons, horse armor, and anvils.
- Find the Dragon Upgrade Smithing Template in End City chests and duplicate it with popped chorus fruit and obsidian.
- Use a dragon shield with a shorter disable cooldown, a dragon sword with extra melee reach, and a dragon chestplate that can be combined with an elytra.
- Upgrade a mace into a more durable dragon mace that grants knockback resistance in the main hand.
- Extend shield cooldowns after a successful shield disable with the dragon axe.
- Use Riptide with the dragon trident in any NeoForge liquid.
- Repair and combine gear in the dragon anvil for 80% of the normal cost, up to 30 levels by default.
- Adjust drops, gear stats, and combat bonuses with the optional Cloth Config screen.
