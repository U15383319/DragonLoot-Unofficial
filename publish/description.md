# DragonLoot Onyx

An unofficial NeoForge port of [DragonLoot](https://github.com/GitPois1x/DragonLoot), originally released for Fabric by **Pois1x** and **Globox_Z**. Ported and maintained independently by **Gvxwsur**. Defeat the Ender Dragon to collect scales and upgrade your gear.

## Differences from the original

- Requires Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.266 or later in the 21.1 series, and Java 21.
- Dragon upgrades use a dedicated smithing template found in End City treasure chests. Duplicate it with seven popped chorus fruit, an obsidian block, and an existing template. Recipes that accept Netherite Plus gear in the Fabric version use vanilla gear here.
- Adds a dragon shield that recovers in 40% of the standard disable time by default.
- The dragon sword has 0.5 blocks of extra melee reach by default.
- The dragon mace grants 10% knockback resistance in the main hand by default.
- The dragon axe adds 1 second to the cooldown of shields it disables by default.
- The dragon anvil charges 80% of the normal cost, up to 30 levels by default.
- Adjust gear stats, combat bonuses, anvil costs, and dragon scale drops in `config/dragonloot-common.toml`. [Cloth Config 15 for NeoForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cloth-config/files/5623420) adds an optional in-game settings screen.
