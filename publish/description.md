# DragonLoot Onyx

An unofficial Forge port of [DragonLoot](https://github.com/GitPois1x/DragonLoot), originally released for Fabric by **Pois1x** and **Globox_Z**. Ported and maintained independently by **Gvxwsur**. Defeat the Ender Dragon to collect scales and upgrade your gear.

## Differences from the original

- Requires Minecraft 1.16.5, Forge 36.2.42 or later in the 36.x series, and Java 11.
- Dragon upgrades use a dedicated smithing template found in End City treasure chests. Duplicate it with seven chorus fruit and a purpur block. Recipes that accept Netherite Plus gear in the Fabric version use vanilla gear here.
- Adds a dragon shield that recovers in 40% of the standard disable time by default.
- The dragon sword has 0.5 blocks of extra melee reach by default.
- Adjust gear stats, combat bonuses, the dragon anvil's level cap, and dragon scale drops in `config/dragonloot-common.toml`. [Cloth Config for Forge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cloth-config/files/5729083) adds an optional in-game settings screen.
